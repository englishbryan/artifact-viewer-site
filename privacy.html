Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Artifact Viewer was built from the ground up to respect your personal privacy. We believe your documents, code, and notes are yours alone.

1. No Data Collection or Tracking

Artifact Viewer does not collect, track, store, or sell any personal information, usage metrics, diagnostic telemetry, or analytics. There are no third-party SDKs, tracking pixels, advertising frameworks, or crash reporters embedded in the app.

2. Document Storage and iCloud Sync

All files you import (HTML web pages, PDFs, Markdown notes, images, stylesheets, scripts) and their reading states (such as scroll positions and dark mode preferences) remain stored locally on your device.

When you enable iCloud, your documents and app data synchronize exclusively using Apple's CloudKit private database associated with your personal Apple Account. The developers cannot access, read, view, or decrypt your files or iCloud data.

3. Network Usage

Artifact Viewer operates completely offline. The only network activity performed by the app is downloading public CDN assets (such as fonts or JavaScript libraries) that an HTML file you choose to open explicitly requests, in order to cache an offline copy on your device for future use. The app never makes any network connections to our own servers or any third-party endpoints.

4. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact support via email at bryanenglish@mac.com.
