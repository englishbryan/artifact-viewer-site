Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add files to my library?

Tap the + button in the top right corner of the library screen to open Apple's Files picker. You can select single .html pages, .pdf files, .md notes, or entire project folders. You can also use the iOS Share Sheet from Safari or Files to send documents directly to Artifact Viewer.

How do linked files work?

If an HTML file or Markdown note contains relative links to other documents (such as another page or a linked PDF), simply import both files or import their parent folder. Linked files automatically tuck under the parent card and open seamlessly over it.

Can I view password-protected PDFs?

Yes. When you tap a locked PDF, Artifact Viewer prompts for the password. You can optionally save the password to your secure Apple Keychain so it unlocks automatically in the future.

How does iCloud sync work?

Artifact Viewer uses Apple's private CloudKit database. As long as you are signed in to iCloud on your devices, your library and saved reading positions sync automatically between your iPhone and iPad without creating an external account.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your local device and within your personal private iCloud account. We run no external servers and never collect your files or personal information.
